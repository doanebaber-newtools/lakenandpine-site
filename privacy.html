SonicBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

SonicBridge is an independent app made by Lake and Pine LLC. It controls Bose SoundTouch speakers on your home network.

What we collect

Nothing. SonicBridge has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours. We never receive your data.

What stays on your device

The speakers you add (name and network address), your saved radio stations, your last played station, and, if you connect Spotify, your Spotify sign-in tokens, stored in your device's secure storage. Removing the app removes all of it.

Who the app talks to

The app connects directly from your phone to:

  • Your speakers, over your local network.
  • The Radio Browser community directory (radio-browser.info), when you search for stations. That service sees your search text and your IP address.
  • Spotify, only if you choose to connect, under Spotify's own privacy policy. You can disconnect Spotify in the app and revoke access from your Spotify account at any time.

Children

SonicBridge isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
